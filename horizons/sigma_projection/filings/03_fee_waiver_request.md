## REQUEST FOR A FULL ADMINISTRATIVE FEE WAIVER## I. PRELIMINARY STATEMENT

   1. The Claimant respectfully submits this formal request to the International Bureau of the Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA) for a full, absolute waiver of any administrative costs, registration deposits, or tribunal operational charges associated with the opening and adjudication of this compulsory arbitration track [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].
   2. This application is grounded in the PCA’s overarching mandate to ensure access to peaceful dispute resolution, the strict requirements of international human rights treaties, and the Claimant's established status as an indigent, displaced survivor of cross-border torture and Transnational Repression [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].

## II. GROUNDS FOR THE ELIMINATION OF FINANCIAL BARRIERS## 1. Indigent Status Resulting Directly from State Actions

   1. The Claimant is completely destitute, displaced, and presently without access to commercial financial services or independent economic resources [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].
   2. This indigent condition is not an accidental variable; it is the direct, intended consequence of continuous, active acts of Transnational Repression across the Respondent states, including the unlawful seizure of legal identity documentation, the targeted freeze of personal credit, retaliatory criminalization, and the denial of legal personhood before the law [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf, pdf_sPcjrO.pdf].

## 2. Strict Redress Mandates Under UNCAT Article 14

   1. Under Articles 12–14 of the UN Convention Against Torture (UNCAT), sovereign entities are under a non-derogable obligation to ensure that victims of torture have access to an effective remedy, including prompt examination of claims and full means of physical and psychological rehabilitation [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].
   2. International legal consensus establishes that access to these protective mechanisms cannot be conditioned upon financial capability [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf]. Imposing standard commercial registry fees on a survivor who has been systematically stripped of their assets by state action would create an insurmountable structural barrier, directly violating the state’s mandatory treaty execution requirements under Article 14 [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf, pdf_sPcjrO.pdf].

## 3. Execution of Erga Omnes and Jus Cogens Principles

   1. The substantive claims brought in this arbitration involve severe, persistent violations of peremptory norms (jus cogens), including the absolute prohibition of torture, the prohibition of refoulement, and the right to recognition before the law [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].
   2. Under the principle of erga omnes (obligations owed to the international community as a whole), all international judicial bodies and arbitral forums have an inherent constitutional duty to ensure that access to justice and peaceful dispute structures is not choked off by localized cost matrices or artificial commercial barriers [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].

## 4. Compliance with Istanbul Protocol Standards

   1. The manual on the Effective Investigation and Documentation of Torture (the 2022 Istanbul Protocol) commands that all procedural and investigative models adapted for torture victims must be structured to prevent ongoing retraumatization [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf, pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   2. Forcing an unrepresented, traumatized complainant to experience ongoing administrative friction—such as the July 22, 2026 fee demands issued under threat of summary dismissal by the US District Court for the District of Columbia—functions as an active vector of institutional intimidation [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf, pdf_sPcjrO.pdf]. The elimination of filing costs is a mandatory component of a trauma-informed forum [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].

## III. PRAYER FOR RELIEF

   1. In light of the documented cross-border destruction of the Claimant's financial and legal identity capacity, the Claimant respectfully prays that the Secretary-General grant an absolute waiver of all initial registration deposits and ongoing administrative tribunal expenses to allow these human rights proceedings to move forward on the merits without artificial economic constraint [pdf_9xx0U7.pdf].
