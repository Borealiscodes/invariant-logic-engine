## I. DEMAND FOR ARBITRATION
The Claimant respectfully submits this Notice of Arbitration to the International Bureau of the Permanent Court of Arbitration pursuant to Article 3 of the PCA Arbitration Rules 2012 [pdf_qh_98X.pdf]. This proceeding is initiated under the founding 1899 Hague Peace Conference mandate to secure a neutral, competent international forum where the internal domestic judicial frameworks of the Respondent States have collapsed or proven themselves structurally unwilling or unable to protect fundamental human rights [pdf_qh_98X.pdf, pdf_ui9Phv.pdf].
## II. JURISDICTION AND COGNIZABLE MATTERS
The jurisdiction of the Arbitral Tribunal is directly activated by jus cogens peremptory norms, erga omnes obligations, and the express mandates of international human rights conventions [pdf_qh_98X.pdf, pdf_9xx0U7.pdf]. The Claimant has established an unalterable, consistent record across the Respondents' domestic administrative bodies, proving a systematic pattern of procedural bad faith, administrative attrition, and a total failure of domestic remedies [pdf_qh_98X.pdf, pdf_ui9Phv.pdf]:

   1. Failure of the Irish Domestic Forum: The Workplace Relations Commission (Case No. ADJ-00068065) remains deadlocked [pdf_rN0GnW.pdf]. Concurrently, the Office of the Protected Disclosures Commissioner (OPDC) issued a formal exclusion on June 25, 2026, asserting that disclosures regarding foreign-state intelligence refoulement cannot be assessed due to a lack of local "worker status," directly violating the territorial investigative mandates of UNCAT Article 12 [pdf_DaeGwh.pdf].
   2. Failure of the New Zealand Domestic Forum: The High Court Wellington Registry Office deployed local formatting Rule 1.21(1) on June 9, 2026, to physically reject the filing of Hedling v. The Governor General, actively insulating public defenders from universal jurisdiction torture liability under the Crimes of Torture Act 1989 [pdf_ui9Phv.pdf, pdf_qzSYUi.pdf].
   3. Failure of the United States Domestic Forum: The US District Court for the District of Columbia (Case No. 1:26-cv-00261-UNA) executed a financial chokehold on July 22, 2026, issuing dozens of payment demands for commercial filing fees to an indigent torture survivor under explicit threat of summary dismissal for failure to prosecute [pdf_sPcjrO.pdf].

## III. THE DOCTRINE OF CONFLICT BY APPLICABILITY (NEMO JUDEX)
The Claimant notes that any domestic statutory gatekeeping provisions, executive barriers, or attorney-general filing consent rules within the Commonwealth structures are legally void under the overriding principle of Nemo judex in causa sua (no one should be a judge in their own cause) [pdf_qh_98X.pdf, pdf_ui9Phv.pdf]. Because the gatekeeping authorities—including the Attorney General and the Crown Prosecution Service—are themselves directly implicated or conflicted in the concealment of cross-border human rights violations, their administrative authority to block adjudication collapses, rendering international intervention mandatory [pdf_ui9Phv.pdf].

The Claimant asserts that the Respondents have breached, and continue to breach, binding international treaty obligations:

   1.    2.
