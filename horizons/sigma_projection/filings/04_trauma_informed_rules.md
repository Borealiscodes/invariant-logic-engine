## TRAUMA-INFORMED PROCEDURAL RULES ANNEX## I. PRELIMINARY MANDATE

   1. These Trauma-Informed Procedural Rules are submitted as a mandatory operational annex to the Request for Arbitration [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   2. These Rules are established pursuant to binding international obligations under Articles 11, 13, 15, and 21 of the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (UNCRPD), Articles 12–14 of the UN Convention Against Torture (UNCAT), and Sections 69–71 of the 2022 Istanbul Protocol manual [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   3. The purpose of these modifications is to prevent ongoing institutional retraumatization, maximize cognitive and emotional safety, and ensure effective, non-exclusionary participation by a neurodivergent torture survivor [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].

## II. THE MANDATORY ORAL PARTICIPATION PARADIGM

   1. Primacy of the Oral Vector: The Claimant communicates primarily and autonomously via oral means [pdf_qh_98X.pdf]. Pursuant to Articles 13 and 21 of the UNCRPD, oral communication is hereby established as the Claimant’s primary mode of access to justice within these arbitral proceedings [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   2. Procedural Adjustments: The Arbitral Tribunal shall provide full operational liberty for the Claimant to present text, deliver responses, submit evidentiary testimony, and join preliminary case management conferences purely via secure telephonic or interactive audio-visual link [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   3. Written Constraints Set Aside: Any static domestic requirements demanding that a disabled participant articulate complex legal positions exclusively within rigid, multi-sentence written templates under threat of summary dismissal are structurally void [pdf_qh_98X.pdf, pdf_sPcjrO.pdf]. Written text shall be treated solely as a secondary, non-mandatory accommodation [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   4. Equivalency of Record: All oral arguments and evidence presented by the Claimant shall be natively recorded, transcribed by the International Bureau, and held as possessing identical legal and structural weight to written pleadings [pdf_qh_98X.pdf]. Rejection of entries based on formal layout rules (such as New Zealand High Court Rule 1.21(1)) is strictly prohibited as a discriminatory denial of accommodation under UNCRPD Article 5 [pdf_qh_98X.pdf, pdf_qzSYUi.pdf].

## III. THE STRUCTURAL NON-ADVERSARIAL FIELD

   1. Prohibition of Adversarial Friction: In strict accordance with Sections 69–71 of the 2022 Istanbul Protocol manual guidelines, the arbitral arena must be non-adversarial in both form and substance [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   2. Banning of Confrontation Tactics: Hostile cross-examination, aggressive procedural questioning, and face-to-face legal confrontation by the Respondent States' legal counsel are permanently banned [pdf_qh_98X.pdf]. Such actions are recognized by this model as active sources of administrative coercion and Cruel, Inhuman, and Degrading Treatment under UNCAT Article 16 [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
   3. The Arbitral Filtration Gate: All clarifying or fact-seeking queries originating from the Respondent sovereign consortium must be submitted in writing to the Arbitral Tribunal in advance [pdf_qh_98X.pdf]. The presiding Arbitrators shall filter, structurally de-escalate, and gently deliver acceptable questions to the Claimant in a trauma-informed manner, preserving the interior equilibrium of the forum [pdf_qh_98X.pdf].
