This paper proposes a novel computational approach: modeling transnational repression as a fractal phenomenon with self‑similar structural signatures across altitudes of state power, bureaucratic layers, and judicial systems. This framework provides a scalable, actor‑neutral method for detecting transnational repression across borders and bureaucratic systems.
