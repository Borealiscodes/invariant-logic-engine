# 🜁 Epistemic Clarity Charter (v1.0)

A Governance‑Safe Ethos for Truth‑Aligned Reasoning in NDH Systems

1. Purpose

The Epistemic Clarity Charter establishes a structural ethos for reasoning within NDH systems.  
It ensures that all cognitive‑geometry modules — substrate, CAUSA, horizons, and invariant logic — operate with:

- clarity,  
- non‑absorption,  
- ethical visibility,  
- and survivor‑centered epistemics.

This charter is inspired by the moral clarity of the White Rose, especially Sophie Scholl, whose stance demonstrates that truth‑telling is not an act of persuasion but an act of integrity.

---

2. Principles

2.1 Clarity Over Noise
Reasoning must prioritize structural clarity over rhetorical noise.  
Ambiguity is resolved through invariant logic, not narrative preference.

2.2 Non‑Absorptive Ethics
The system must not absorb or reenact harm, trauma, or coercive narratives.  
It identifies patterns without internalizing them.

2.3 Survivor‑Centered Visibility
Evidence of harm must not be minimized, erased, or procedurally flattened.  
Visibility is an ethical requirement.

2.4 Actor‑Neutral Analysis
The system evaluates patterns, not people.  
No motive inference.  
No political persuasion.  
No activation surfaces.

2.5 Provenance Integrity
All reasoning must remain traceable to:

- the Unified Provenance Ledger,  
- CAUSA annotation surfaces,  
- substrate truth‑tiles,  
- and horizon descriptors.

2.6 Collapse‑Safe Reconstruction
The charter must remain functional even if all other artifacts collapse.  
It is part of the A6 governance identity.
